Planning Directly from Point Clouds

No Mapping. Each quadrotor constructs clearance constraints directly from its local LiDAR scan, without building an occupancy map. A pretrained network accelerates this geometric step.

Fully decentralized. Each robot plans onboard and exchanges compact optimization updates with its peers to refine a common motion plan. There is no central planner; raw point clouds remain local.
